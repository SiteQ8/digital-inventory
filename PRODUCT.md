# Product

## Who it is for

Any adult with a phone and a few accounts, and the family who will one day have to deal with them. Not specialists.

## The job

Leave the family one sheet that says what exists, who receives it, what to do with it and where the way in is kept, and tell the owner what to set up today at each provider.

## Principles

1. No secrets in the tool. It records where a secret is kept, never the secret.
2. Nothing leaves the browser. No account, no server, no tracking.
3. Official routes over workarounds. For money, the official inheritance route only.
4. It organizes and records. It gives no religious or legal ruling.
5. Arabic first, English equal.
